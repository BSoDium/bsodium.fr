# Landing hero

The home page (`/`): the pixel globe with a typographic hero over it. Design source: the owner's canvas boards "home-rest" and "home-touched" (2026-10-12; 1440 x 900, dark scheme; both schemes are built from the app tokens, nothing is copied from the boards' hex values). All type, no boxes, no ornament; the accent appears once (a row or header item under the pointer or the keyboard). Identity change (2026-10-13): the big type is the owner's NAME, not the project's code name, and the four rows replace the header's section links on this page.

Round 3 (2026-10-13, owner feedback items 3 and 10): the home page opens fully zoomed OUT and, at rest, shows the planet with **orbiting type** (a mono ring of the identity and a heavy two-line caption that wraps the planet, in 3D, `docs/web-architecture.md` "Orbiting type") instead of boxes and labels; the caption replaces the how-to line; the planet is **lifted** clear of the name; and the collapse **shrinks the name** into the small line. See "Showcase" below.

Code: `app/components/landing-hero.tsx` (the markup and the name's travel), `app/lib/hero-machine.ts` (the state machine and `showcaseOf`, pure), `app/hooks/use-landing-hero.ts` (wiring to the shell: the machine, the showcase signal and the measured cover), `app/routes/shell.tsx` (mounts it), the "Landing hero" block at the end of `app/app.css`. Check: `scripts/home/hero.mjs`; the globe's side: `scripts/globe/ring.mjs`.

## Structure

```
<div data-slot="landing-hero" data-hero="rest | collapsed | away" data-world="true | false">
  h1.hero-name        "Elliot" (.hero-given, 800) + "Négrel-Jerzy" (.hero-family .type-thin, 200), Bricolage, .type-display-xl; the page's h1
  .hero-lead
    p.hero-intro      who and what: "Front-end and UX software engineer based in Paris." + span.hero-intro-more " Data visualisation, geospatial and cartography interfaces, design systems, computer graphics." (the second sentence goes in a very short window)
    p.hero-hint       how: "Pick a point on the globe, or start from a list." (muted, a step smaller; gone in a short window; without scripts: "Start from a list."). While the globe has the orbiting caption (`data-orbit` "pending" or "true") it is visually hidden and stays in the accessibility tree: the caption is the cue, this is the text.
    button.hero-search  [data-needs-script] the search icon (control size), "Search places and entries" (mono 13), the key hint (command icon + K, or Ctrl K); opens the existing palette
  nav.hero-list       aria-label "Primary" (the home page's navigation; the header has none here)
    ul > li > a.hero-row  Projects, Articles, Artworks, Poetry: hairline rows, Bricolage 30 / 300, an arrow icon at 1.1 em (33 px)
  p.hero-mini         aria-hidden: the small name (span.hero-mini-given 800 + span.hero-mini-family 200: where the big name's two halves travel to) and "Drag to explore. Back to start after a moment."
```

Layout (wide): intro, hint and search left (470 px), the four rows right (360 px), the name under them: ONE line from 48 rem, **deliberately not the full width** (owner, round 2: "clearly narrower"): it takes `--hero-span` 0.72 of the viewport (`--hero-size` = 100vw x 0.72 / `--hero-fit` 6.6 em, the name's measured width at 800 / 200: 157 px at 1440, which is 1037 px, 72 %; 737 px at 1024; 565 px, 74 %, at 768 where the optical size is a little wider), never over a third of the height nor 30 rem. Weights: the given name is the heavy half (800, set at -0.02 em: at the name's -0.055 em the three l, l, i of "Elliot" touched), the family name the thin half (200, -0.055 em). The hyphen of the family name has its own span (`.hero-hyphen`, 0.045 em of margin). Gutters 56 px (`clamp(1rem, 3.9vw, 3.5rem)`). Under 48 rem it stacks in two lines: the given name over the family name, whose width is what fills the row there (`--hero-fit` 4.7, gutter to gutter: 76 px at 390), then intro, hint, search, rows. Wide and short (under 34 rem tall, from 48 rem wide): the rows in two columns, the name capped at 26 vh and the hint dropped (the globe is in plain view; the line saved is room for it).

**The whole block is always in the window** (review of 2026-10-13: at 667 x 375, 720 x 450 = a 1440 x 900 window at 200 % zoom, 320 x 568 and 320 x 480 the stacked block was 510 to 540 px tall, bottom-anchored in an `overflow: hidden` main, so the name was at negative y). From 320 x 480 and from 568 x 320 up:
- The stacked name's size is also what the height leaves (`--hero-size` takes `(100dvh - --hero-fixed - --hero-clear - safe area - 1rem) / 1.9`; `--hero-fixed` is what the block holds besides the name, per tier: 26.5 rem, 24.5 rem without the hint, 17 rem with the rows in two columns, 13.5 rem without the second sentence), never over the width fit and 24 vh, never under 1.75 rem.
- Tiers under 48 rem: up to 40 rem tall the how-to line is gone; up to 36 rem the four rows are two columns of 44 px targets; under 30 rem wide and up to 28 rem tall only who the owner is stays of the intro (the second sentence is its own span, `.hero-intro-more`).
- A phone on its side, 30 to 48 rem wide and up to 34 rem tall: the wide layout in small (intro and search left, the two-by-two rows right, the name on ONE line across the bottom at 72 % of the width, 26 vh at most); at 22 rem tall, or 28 rem under 40 rem wide, also without the second sentence.
- The dev badge raises the block by 4.75 rem under 48 rem (`--hero-clear`): the tiers hold with it. Below 320 x 480 (320 x 320 and the like, which no phone has) the block can still overflow by up to 25 px: not supported.
- The sizes are measured in `scripts/home/hero.mjs` (15 shapes, light and dark, top and bottom of the block's box against the window, the globe reachable above it). DOM order is reading order (name, intro, search, rows); the grid places the name last on a wide screen.

The block is bottom-anchored and rises by the credits line's height plus what the "y" hangs under the line box (`padding-bottom: clear + 0.15 * name size`), so no letter is ever over the credits line (bottom right, 8 to 30 px from the bottom) or, in development, the content badge. The hero is `pointer-events: none`; only the search line and the rows take the pointer, so the globe stays draggable and zoomable beneath all the rest. A page-colour fade (`.landing-hero::before`, no blur) sits behind the thin type.

## Showcase (round 3)

While the hero is `rest` at the world view (`showcaseOf(snapshot, cover)`: `state === "rest" && world`) the home screen is a **showcase**: nothing else of the globe's overlay is there to compete with the type.

- **Start zoomed out.** The page opens at the globe's lowest zoom (`GlobeRenderer.startView`: `minZoom`, the whole-globe fit minus the room the ring needs, 0.5 levels at every checked viewport) instead of the fit. `GlobeViewState.zoom` still reports 0 below the fit, so the saved view, the world view of the machine and the idle rotation are unchanged. The ring existed before only when the visitor zoomed out; at the fit the group boxes made it absent, which is why it never showed on load.
- **What changes.** The boxes and labels fade out (`LodTree.setSuppressed`: every node's target goes OFF and runs by its own timed opacity, `FADE_MS` 200 ms, no re-planning) while the type fades in (`showcaseFade`, the same primitive and duration, times the zoom regime). The hidden boxes are inert: a node that is off takes no pick (`pickAlpha`), and `pickAt` answers null while the showcase is on. The first gesture (a press or a wheel over the globe: the engine's own capture listener, and the hero's `onInteract`) ends it at once: the boxes come back and the press that began it selects nothing (a box nobody could see). The places list, the keyboard and the URL paths are untouched (a place opening takes the hero to `collapsed` and ends it).
- **The planet is lifted.** While the showcase is on the planet's centre goes UP by what makes its lowest point clear the name (the hero measures `name.offsetTop` from the page's bottom: `LandingHero` `onCover`, a `ResizeObserver`, layout offsets so the name's own transform never changes it) plus 14 px, as far as the room above allows (`liftFor`, `engine/lift.ts`: the highest thing, the planet or the ring's top, keeps 14 px from the top edge). At 1440 x 900 the planet's centre moves 46 px up and the planet sits between the intro and the rows (they overlap it on its sides as before); on a phone, where the stacked block covers two thirds of the page, the planet goes as high as the room allows and is clear of the name. It follows the cover when it changes (a resize, the tiers of the CSS, a font arriving), tweened like the panel's inset (`TUNING.insetMs`, `INSET_EASE`), and comes down in the same time when the showcase ends. Reduced motion: at once. The lowest zoom, the fit and the framing clamps use the same centre (`liftBuf` in every projection: the camera's view offset, the markers, the label tree's `centreY`, the sky's disc).
- **Words.** The ring is the identity line unchanged (`ringPhrases(SITE)`, budget logic of `ring-text.ts`: name, role, the specialities that fit, place). The caption is `SITE_CALL` in `lib/site.ts`: "Drag to explore" over "Pick a place" (interface copy, not a fact; no code name can reach it).
- **Accessibility.** The orbiting type is a canvas with no text in the page (not in the accessibility tree, not selectable). The how-to line is visually hidden (not removed) while the type is there; without scripts (`scripting: none`), without WebGL, with `?no-ring` and on the empty archive (`data-orbit="false"`) it shows as before. The hero tells nothing to a screen reader that it did not before.
- **Off switches.** `?no-ring` (the ring, the caption, the lowest zoom below the fit, the showcase): the page is the previous one, the boxes stay and the hint shows. A debug page (`?globe-debug`) has it off unless `?ring`.

## States

| State | When | What shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rest` | load; or idle at the world view after a gesture | the whole hero |
| `collapsed` | after a gesture on the globe; or whenever the view is not the world view (zoomed in, a place open) | the small line bottom-left (left 40 px, bottom 32 px: the name in Bricolage 30: "Elliot" at 800 and "Négrel-Jerzy" at 200, as in the hero, and the hint). The h1 stays in the accessibility tree at opacity 0; the search line and the rows are `visibility: hidden` (not tab stops). The hint is shown only while the way back is real (`data-world="true"`) |
| `away` | a place or an entry is open | nothing is shown or exposed (instant); the name renders as a `p`, so the panel's title is the only h1 |

Transitions: opacity and transform only (visibility follows the fade), `--duration-base` (220 ms) with `--ease-standard`; `prefers-reduced-motion`: none (instant). No JS animation loop; a state change costs one React re-render of the hero, and only on a gesture start, the world view being left or reached, or the idle timer.

**The name's travel (owner, item 10: "shrinking the text, not just fading").** On `rest` -> `collapsed` the big name's two halves (`.hero-given`, `.hero-family`, `inline-block`, `transform-origin: 0 0`) shrink and travel to where the small line has them (`.hero-mini-given`, `.hero-mini-family`, bottom-left), and `collapsed` -> `rest` is the same motion reversed. A FLIP done once at the state change (`travelName` in `landing-hero.tsx`, in a layout effect, before paint): the two text boxes of each half are measured (`Range.getBoundingClientRect`: two reads, no loop), the scale is the ratio of their widths (ONE uniform scale per half, about 0.19 at 1440 px) and the translation puts the box's corner on the other's; then `Element.animate` with two keyframes (`transform` only) at `NAME_TRAVEL_MS` 360 ms and the app's standard ease (the panel's slide), run by the compositor: no font size, no layout, no per-frame JS, no `will-change` (the animation itself promotes the layer for its length and is cancelled on finish). Meanwhile the rest of the block (intro, hint) fades and slides as before and the search line and the rows hide as before; the name's own opacity is 1 for the travel and drops (90 ms) in the last 90 ms while the small line's comes up (90 ms) under it, so the h1 is visible for the whole travel. Back: the small line goes first (60 ms) and the name travels out of its place. An animation still running is reversed, not restarted. Reduced motion: no animation (the CSS is instant, as before). The h1 stays in the accessibility tree (opacity 0 when collapsed), the focus hold, the tab order and the server markup are unchanged; `hero.mjs` checks the hydration warnings.

The machine (`createHeroMachine`): `rest` goes to `collapsed` on `interact()` or when `setWorld(false)`; `collapsed` goes to `rest` after `HERO_IDLE_MS` (8000, the same delay as the globe's idle rotation) without an input, only while the world view holds. Every input restarts the clock; leaving the world view stops it and coming back restarts it. At most one timer, none at rest. **The clock is held while the keyboard focus is in the header (`[data-slot="site-header"]`) or in an open dialog (the search palette)**: the way back removes the header (the rows are the sections then), so a hero that came back under a focused header link, or under the palette whose opener is the header's trigger, left the focus on `body`. `HeroMachine.hold(held)`, fed by `watchFocusHold` (`hooks/use-landing-hero.ts`: `focusin` and `focusout` on the document); releasing starts a whole idle period. "World view": `GlobeViewState.zoom <= 0.03` and no street scale (`isWorldView`; the idle rotation's own slack, 0.15 zoom levels of a range of about 5.3), with no place or entry open.

## Hooks into the globe

- **The showcase** (round 3): `GlobeProps.showcase`, a signal (`{ get(), subscribe() }`, `useLandingHero().showcase`) over the machine and the measured cover: `{ on, coverBottom }`. The globe subscribes, so a gesture or the idle period moves the globe's state without the shell or the globe's props rendering. `GlobeProps.captionPhrases` are the caption's words, `GlobeProps.onOrbit(available)` tells the hero whether the orbiting type exists (it hides its own how-to line only then). Engine side: `GlobeRenderer.setShowcase`, `setCover`, `LodTree.setSuppressed`.
- **A gesture**: `GlobeProps.onInteract` (`globe/types.ts`), implemented in `globe/globe-canvas.tsx`: two passive capture listeners (`pointerdown`, `wheel`) on the globe's own stage, which holds the canvas, so pointer presses (drag, tap on a place, a pinch's fingers) and wheel (including the trackpad pinch) count, hovering does not, the app's overlays and the credits control are outside it, the globe's own motion (rotation, flights, inertia) is not input. The globe has no keyboard zoom (the canvas is not focusable, the places list is the keyboard path), so there is none to hook. No engine file changed.
- **The view**: the shell already receives every reported view (`onViewChange`); `useLandingHero().onView` compares it with the world view and tells the machine only when that changes.
- **The search**: `openSearch()` (`components/search-palette.tsx`) dispatches a window event the palette (mounted once by the navigation) listens to. The hero's line is a `button` (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-keyshortcuts`), not a second search; Cmd/Ctrl-K is unchanged.

## Header

Restyled with the hero (the board's header): `Globe` (the home page; accessible name "Globe, Elliot Négrel-Jerzy home"), Projects, Articles, Artworks, Poetry in mono capitals (`.nav-link`: 12 px, tracking 0.14em and 44 px between items from a 44 rem wide row, shrinking by container width down to 11 px / 6 px on a 320 px phone), the current one in the foreground and underlined, the rest muted, the pointer or keyboard turning an item to `--signal-text`. Where the icon trigger is hidden (row under 28 rem: a phone) the same control is one word, "Search" (`data-slot="search-trigger-compact"`, 44 px target).

**On the home page there is no header at all while the hero is at rest** (owner, 2026-10-13, twice: first "no section tabs, the hero's rows are the sections", then "no top bar element at all: the hero's own search line has the search and the key hint"). `Navbar` renders no `<header>`: no bar, no tabs, no search trigger, no fade. What keeps the page accessible: the skip links are unchanged; the first controls in the tab order are the hero's search line and its four rows; the hero's list is a `nav` with `aria-label="Primary"` (the only Primary navigation in the accessibility tree); Cmd/Ctrl-K opens the palette as everywhere (the palette is mounted by `Navbar` beside the header, not inside it, so it also stays open when the header comes or goes). **The header returns whenever the hero is not at rest** (tabs and search, with the plain key hint): collapsed after a gesture, or zoomed in (the rows are `visibility: hidden` then, and zoomed in the hero never returns on its own, so without this the visitor would have no way to the lists but the search); it goes again when the hero returns. Mechanism: `LandingHero` publishes `state === "rest"` to `lib/hero-rest.ts` (a tiny external store) and `Navbar` reads it, because the header is a sibling of the shell in the root layout. The server and the first client render say "at rest", like the hero's server markup, so nothing differs at hydration. Every other route, including a place or an entry opened from the globe, has the header unchanged. **The header that a gesture brings ignores the pointer for a moment** (2026-10-13, found in round 3 of the hero, present since the header came back on collapse): on a phone a tap on the globe collapses the hero in the same task as the press, the header row is under the finger when it lifts, and the tap's click used to activate the link there (a tap at the very top navigated to "Articles"). `Navbar`'s `useGestureGuard` sets `data-guard` on a header that appears because the hero left rest on `/`, and `app.css` makes its controls `pointer-events: none` until the pointer sequence in progress has ended plus `GESTURE_GUARD_MS` (350 ms); the click lands on the globe, which has none. The header stays visible; keyboard focus and assistive technology are untouched (they do not use the pointer). `hero.mjs` section 11 taps where the link will appear (390 x 844, touch) and requires the page to stay on `/`, then a real tap on the link after the guard to navigate.

## Empty archive

With no published place the hero is also the empty home (it replaced the old `ArchiveEmpty` plate, since removed): the hint reads "No place is published yet: start from a list." (under who the owner is). The hero shows no count or number.
